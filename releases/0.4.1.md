## Changes

- A profile whose server pushes `redirect-gateway` is a full tunnel with OpenVPN before 2.7 too, such as the 2.6 that Ubuntu 24.04 ships. Before, only OpenVPN 2.7 told the helper about the directive, and with 2.6 the tunnel was a split tunnel and the traffic around it was not sent through the VPN

The macOS app is the same as in 0.4.0, and its helper differs only in this.
