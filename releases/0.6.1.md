## Changes

- Windows: one installer for every language, `Plaitway-0.6.1-x64.msi`, in place of the two of 0.6.0. The setup dialog is in English; the app follows the language of Windows, and **Settings › Language** chooses System default, English or 繁體中文. The choice is read when the app starts, and **Restart Plaitway** starts it again at once; profiles that are on stay on
- Scoop installs the same package: `scoop update plaitway`

The macOS app and the Linux packages are the same as in 0.6.0.
