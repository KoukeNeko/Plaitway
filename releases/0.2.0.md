## Changes

First public release.

- Several profiles connected at the same time; the sidebar order is the priority, a full tunnel is held by one profile and the others stand by
- Routes and DNS written ahead to a journal and repaired after a network change, a new default gateway or a wake from sleep
- A window with a profile sidebar and five pages per profile: Overview with traffic, Routes and DNS, Logs, Configuration, Settings; a Diagnostics page with stale routes and a report to copy
- Editing OpenVPN and WireGuard profiles in the window or with `plaitway edit`, with private keys hidden behind placeholders until asked for
- Auto-connect, on-demand activation by network type, and leaving the private ranges out of a WireGuard tunnel
- The `plaitway` command line tool, in the app bundle under `Contents/Resources/bin`
- English and Traditional Chinese
