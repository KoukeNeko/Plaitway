## Changes

- Diagnostics and Settings are at the bottom of the sidebar, below the profiles
- Settings is a page of the main window, not a window of its own: Settings… (⌘,), the menu bar item and the Dock menu open it there, and its page has no page switcher in the toolbar
- The configuration editor: the margin's separator no longer runs up through the bar above the editor, the margin has a tint of its own, the line of the insertion point is marked, and the number of the line the helper refused is red
