## Changes

- Plaitway runs on Linux with systemd: a GTK 4 app (`plaitway-app`), the `plaitway` command and the helper `plaitwayd` as a system service, in one `.deb` for amd64 and arm64. WireGuard and OpenVPN profiles work as on macOS, with the routes owned by the helper and a tunnel's DNS set per interface in systemd-resolved
- The Linux app keeps credentials in the desktop keyring, shows the state in the tray through StatusNotifierItem, and starts or restarts the helper after a polkit prompt. It is in English and Traditional Chinese

The macOS app and helper are the same as in 0.3.5.
